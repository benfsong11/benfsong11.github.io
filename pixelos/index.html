What it does

Eight tools

Pencil, eraser, fill, eyedropper, line, rectangle, move and rectangular selection. Integer Bresenham, nearest-neighbour zoom — nothing is anti-aliased anywhere.

Layers

Opacity, visibility, reordering and merge down, with region-based undo that stays cheap on a big canvas.

Animation

A timeline of frames, per-frame timing, looping playback and onion skin — warm for the frames behind, cool for the ones ahead.

Export

A frame at 1× to 64×, a sprite sheet as a row, column or grid with optional JSON, or the whole timeline as a looping GIF.

Keyboard first

Every tool is one key away, and the shortcuts work with a Korean input source active. Space-drag to pan, wheel to zoom on the pointer.

Installing

Open the app once from wherever it lands and it offers to move itself into Applications. Say yes and the Pixelos already installed there goes to the Trash, along with the copy you just downloaded — one Pixelos, in the right place. Nothing is deleted outright, and nothing happens until you agree.
